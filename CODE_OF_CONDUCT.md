# Code of Conduct

## AI Process Analyst Community

---

# Our Commitment

AI Process Analyst is committed to fostering an open, welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community.

We believe that collaboration, curiosity, and constructive discussion create better software, stronger ideas, and a more enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

All contributors, regardless of experience level or background, are expected to help maintain a positive and professional environment.

---

# Our Values

We encourage:

- Respectful communication
- Constructive feedback
- Continuous learning
- Knowledge sharing
- Collaboration
- Professional behaviour
- Curiosity and experimentation
- Responsible use of Artificial Intelligence

---

# Expected Behaviour

Community members are encouraged to:

- Treat everyone with respect.
- Be open to different ideas and perspectives.
- Provide constructive technical feedback.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.
- Help newcomers learn.
- Share knowledge generously.
- Be patient during reviews and discussions.
- Assume positive intent.

---

# Unacceptable Behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Personal attacks
- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Offensive language
- Intimidation
- Deliberate misinformation
- Trolling
- Publishing private information without permission
- Disruptive behaviour that negatively impacts the community

---

# Responsible AI

This project places particular importance on responsible use of Artificial Intelligence.

Contributors should:

- Avoid generating misleading information.
- Clearly distinguish AI-generated suggestions from verified business knowledge.
- Maintain transparency where AI is used.
- Prioritize explainability whenever practical.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.

---

# Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it by opening a private discussion with the project maintainer.

Reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially whenever possible.

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

The goal is not punishment, but maintaining a collaborative and respectful community.

---

# A Shared Responsibility

Every contributor plays an important role in creating a welcoming and productive open-source community.

By participating in AI Process Analyst, you agree to uphold these principles and contribute positively to the project.
