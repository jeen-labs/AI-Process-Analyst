# 1. Purpose

The Customer Onboarding process establishes a consistent and controlled approach for registering new customers within the organisation.

The process ensures that customer information is collected, validated, approved, and recorded in accordance with regulatory requirements, internal governance standards, and operational procedures.

A successful onboarding process enables customers to begin using organisational products and services while ensuring compliance with legal, financial, and operational obligations.

The process supports both operational efficiency and regulatory compliance by ensuring that every customer record is complete, accurate, auditable, and approved before activation.

---

# 2. Business Objectives

The objectives of the Customer Onboarding process are to:

- Provide a consistent onboarding experience for all customers.
- Reduce customer onboarding time.
- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Reduce manual processing errors.
- Prevent duplicate customer records.
- Maintain complete audit history.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Support digital onboarding initiatives.
- Ensure all required approvals are completed before activation.

---

# 3. Scope

This process applies to all new customers requesting products or services provided by the organisation.

The process begins when a completed customer application is received and ends when the customer account has been successfully activated or the application has been formally rejected.

This process applies to:

- Individual customers
- Corporate customers
- Domestic customers
- International customers

The process includes:

- Application receipt
- Document verification
- Identity verification
- Compliance review
- Risk assessment
- Management approval
- Customer creation
- Account activation
- Customer notification

---

# 4. Out of Scope

The following activities are not part of this process:

- Customer account maintenance
- Customer offboarding
- Customer complaints
- Product servicing
- Loan approval
- Credit assessment
- Customer relationship management after activation

These activities are governed by separate business processes.

---

# 5. Process Overview

Customer Onboarding transforms a customer application into an active customer record through a controlled sequence of validation, verification, approval, and registration activities.

The process combines automated system validations with manual business reviews to ensure that customer information is complete and compliant before any products or services are made available.

Each activity generates traceable outputs that become inputs to subsequent stages of the process.

Every decision is recorded to support governance, auditing, and regulatory reporting.

The process is designed to minimise operational risk while providing customers with a timely and consistent onboarding experience.

---

# 6. Process Success Criteria

The process is considered successful when:

- Customer identity has been verified.
- Mandatory documentation has been received.
- Compliance screening has been completed.
- Required approvals have been obtained.
- Customer record has been created.
- Customer account has been activated.
- Welcome communication has been issued.
- Audit records have been generated.

Failure to satisfy any mandatory requirement results in rejection, suspension, or escalation of the application.

# 9. Process Inputs

The onboarding process may receive one or more of the following inputs.

| Input                            | Mandatory                |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Customer Application Form        | Yes                      |
| Government Identification        | Yes                      |
| Proof of Address                 | Yes                      |
| Company Registration Certificate | Corporate Customers Only |
| Tax Identification Number        | Yes                      |
| Supporting Business Documents    | Where Applicable         |
| Customer Consent Form            | Yes                      |
| Product Request                  | Yes                      |

Applications missing mandatory inputs cannot proceed to verification.

---

# 10. Process Outputs

Successful completion of the process produces the following outputs.

| Output                  | Description                                  |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Customer Record         | New customer profile                         |
| Customer Identifier     | Unique enterprise customer ID                |
| Active Customer Account | Activated customer account                   |
| Compliance Record       | AML and KYC completion evidence              |
| Audit Trail             | Complete onboarding history                  |
| Welcome Notification    | Customer communication confirming activation |
| Management Reports      | Operational reporting data                   |

These outputs become inputs to downstream business processes such as Account Management, Product Servicing, Customer Support, Billing, and Reporting.
