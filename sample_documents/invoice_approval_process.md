# Invoice Approval Process

## Process Overview

The Invoice Approval Process ensures that all supplier invoices are properly reviewed, validated, and approved before payment processing. This process involves multiple approval levels based on invoice value and includes verification of goods receipt, pricing accuracy, and budget availability.

## Process Owner

Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

## Process Steps

### Step 1: Invoice Receipt

- Invoice received from supplier via email, mail, or electronic submission
- Invoice logged into the Accounts Payable system
- Invoice details captured (supplier name, invoice number, date, amount)

### Step 2: Three-Way Matching

- Invoice matched against Purchase Order (PO)
- Invoice matched against Goods Receipt Note (GRN)
- Verify:
  - Quantities match
  - Prices match
  - Total amounts match
  - PO is valid and not expired

### Step 3: Budget Check

- Verify budget availability for the expense
- Check against departmental budget allocations
- Ensure no budget overruns

### Step 4: Tax Compliance Check

- Verify VAT/GST calculations
- Validate tax invoice requirements
- Check for tax registration numbers

### Step 5: Approval Routing

- **Low Value (< $1,000):** Department Manager approval only
- **Medium Value ($1,000 - $10,000):** Department Manager + Finance Manager
- **High Value (> $10,000):** Department Manager + Finance Manager + CFO

### Step 6: Payment Scheduling

- Determine payment terms (Net 30, Net 60)
- Schedule payment based on due date
- Apply early payment discounts if applicable

### Step 7: Payment Execution

- Payment prepared in banking system
- Payment approved by authorized signatories
- Payment transmitted to bank
- Payment confirmation received

## Key Performance Indicators

- Invoice processing time: < 5 working days
- Approval cycle time: < 48 hours
- Matching accuracy: > 99%
- Early payment discount capture: > 80%

## Exceptions

- Discrepancy in matching → Escalate to Procurement
- Missing PO → Escalate to Requesting Department
- Budget overrun → Escalate to Finance Manager
- Supplier payment disputes → Escalate to Accounts Payable Team

## Systems Involved

- ERP System (for PO and GRN)
- Invoice Management System
- Banking Portal
- Document Management System

## Compliance Requirements

- SOX compliance for financial controls
- Tax authority reporting requirements
- Internal audit requirements
- Segregation of duties controls
