# Purchase Request Process

## What

Standardized process for requesting and approving purchases within the organization.

## Objective

- Ensure all purchases are justified and budgeted
- Maintain procurement compliance
- Track purchase requests from initiation to fulfillment
- Prevent unauthorized spending

## Roles

**Requestor**

- Creates purchase request
- Provides business justification
- Identifies preferred supplier

**Department Manager**

- Reviews business need
- Approves budget allocation
- Validates priority

**Procurement Team**

- Reviews request specifications
- Obtains quotes
- Selects best supplier

**Finance**

- Verifies budget availability
- Handles payment processing
- Manages supplier onboarding

## Process Flow

### 1. Request Creation

- Requestor fills purchase request form
- Includes: item description, quantity, estimated cost, required date
- Attach supporting documentation

### 2. Business Review

- Department Manager review
- Check: Is this necessary? Is there budget?
- Decision: Approve/Return for revisions/Reject

### 3. Procurement Review (if approved)

- Technical review of requirements
- Market research (quotes from 3 suppliers if > $5,000)
- Supplier selection and negotiation

### 4. Purchase Order Creation

- PO generated in procurement system
- PO sent to supplier
- Supplier acknowledges receipt

### 5. Goods Receipt

- Goods received against PO
- Quality inspection (if applicable)
- Receipt recorded in system

### 6. Invoice Processing

- Invoice received and matched against PO and receipt
- Three-way matching completed
- Payment scheduled

## Decision Points

**Budget Check:**

- ✅ Pass → Continue to procurement
- ❌ Fail → Requestor to adjust or secure additional budget

**Quote Evaluation:**

- ✅ Best price/value selected → Proceed to PO
- ❌ Budget exceeds threshold → Additional approval needed

## Performance Metrics

- Request to PO cycle time: Target 3 days
- Requisition accuracy rate: Target 95%
- Purchase order accuracy: Target 98%
- Supplier delivery performance: Target 90% on-time

## System Requirements

- Procurement Management System
- ERP Financials
- Supplier Portal

## Key Controls

- Purchase requests above $5,000 require 3 quotes
- Segregation of duties (requestor ≠ approver)
- All purchases must have valid budget allocation
- Audit trail maintained for all requests

## Escalation Path

1. Missing information → Return to Requestor
2. Urgent requests → Expedite with Manager approval
3. System issues → Contact IT Support
4. Supplier issues → Contact Procurement

## Training Requirements

- All requestors: Basic procurement policy training
- Managers: Approval authority training
- Procurement team: Advanced procurement procedures
